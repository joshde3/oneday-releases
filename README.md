# OneDay

A calm personal daily dashboard for Android. Everything you need to know about today, in one place: the time and weather, today's tasks, things to remember, and your habits.

- **Tasks** with times and reminders
- **Remember** important dates (one-off, weekly, monthly or yearly), with overdue and coming-up nudges
- **Habits** with daily, weekday, weekend or custom schedules
- **Quick notes**, search and a calendar view
- Light, dark and system themes
- Reminders arrive even when the app is closed
- No account needed: your data stays on your device

## Download

Get the latest APK from the [**Releases page**](../../releases/latest).

### Installing

1. Download `OneDay-<version>.apk` on your Android phone or tablet (Android 7.0 or newer).
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**.
3. Tap **Install**.

To update, install the newer APK over the old one. Your data is kept.

## Privacy

OneDay stores everything on your device and has no accounts or analytics. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
