# OneDay Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

OneDay ("the app") is a personal daily dashboard. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## Summary

- Your tasks, reminders, habits and notes **never leave your device**.
- There are **no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking**.
- The app only contacts outside services to show the **weather** and to load its **font**.

## Data stored on your device

Everything you enter (tasks, things to remember, habits, notes and settings) is stored locally in the app's storage on your device. The developer cannot see it, and it is not sent to any server.

You can export a backup or delete all of your data at any time in **Settings → Data & Storage**. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## Location and weather

If you allow location access, the app reads your device's **approximate location** to show local weather. It is not stored by the developer or linked to your identity. Your coordinates are sent to:

- **[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)** (`api.open-meteo.com`), to get the weather forecast.
- **[BigDataCloud](https://www.bigdatacloud.com/privacy)** (`api.bigdatacloud.net`), to look up the name of your city.

If you choose a city manually instead, the city name you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service (`geocoding-api.open-meteo.com`) to find its coordinates, and no device location is used.

Location access is optional. You can deny it or turn it off in your device settings, and the app will use a city you pick instead.

## Fonts

The app loads its typeface from **[Google Fonts](https://policies.google.com/privacy)**. As with any website, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information when the font is downloaded.

## Notifications

Notification permission is used only to show reminders that you scheduled for your tasks, habits and things to remember. Reminders are scheduled on your device by Android. No notification content is sent anywhere.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new "Last updated" date.
